**`intervention_card`: two more read-only situations, `message` and `approval`.** A `message` carries the sender's id, the digest of the message bytes, a one-line subject, a plain-text body, optional `links[]` (at most 5, https only) and an optional `reply` that names how an answer renders (`text` or `choice`) and must answer exactly this message by its digest. An `approval` carries its own id, the digest of what is approved, the operation, the preview shown, its status and times, and no action: the decision stays with the service that owns it. Bodies and previews are plain text: control and format characters are refused, `<` and `>` are allowed. The published schema and TypeScript types list both situations.
