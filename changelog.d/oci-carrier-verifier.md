**Add `zeo_core.release.oci`: bounded offline verification of an OCI image archive.** `verify_oci_carrier` checks an uncompressed OCI layout tar against independently supplied expectations (archive digest and size, entry limit, manifest and config digests, platform). It reads at most the declared size plus one byte, never extracts, decompresses, loads or deploys anything, accepts only regular entries named `oci-layout`, `index.json` or `blobs/sha256/<digest>`, and refuses links, devices, directories, extension headers, duplicate names, nonzero padding or trailing bytes, wrong blob hashes, descriptor sizes and platform mismatches. A verified result is byte and structure integrity only, not signed or admitted provenance. See the how-to for what the admission and actuator layers must still verify.
