**Add `CommitmentCalendar` to `packages/react`: a read-only renderer for the `calendar_view` payload, with its strict browser validator.** `parseCalendar` and `validateCalendar` check the published schema and the model rules the schema cannot express (lateness against its causes, counts, page arithmetic, consecutive dates, item identity, revision order by instant), with the same byte-level strictness as for cards. The schema and the rules are generated from the Python models with a drift test. The renderer shows the original due time, the forecast and the finish time as separate facts and computes nothing. The gallery gains a calendar view, and the browser test checks it for accessibility and phone overflow.
