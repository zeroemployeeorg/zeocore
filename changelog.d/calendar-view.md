**Add the `calendar_view` widget component: the commitment calendar.** `zeo_core.contracts.ui.calendar_views.CalendarView` is a read-only Today or seven-day snapshot of commitments as their owner publishes them. Each item keeps its original due time, current forecast and finish time apart, the owner's status and lateness verdict with its policy text, signed differences, a revision history with a completeness flag, and the owner's legacy id with the canonical identity explicitly unresolved unless registered. A page is a stable, source-ordered cut of one immutable snapshot; its `paging` names the snapshot digest, the whole count, the eligible rows, the offset, the returned rows and the next cursor, so a cut is never silent. The component joins Core's widget profile, so `ui.widget-upsert.schema.json` lists `calendar_view`; the locked schema still refuses it. The payload schema and its TypeScript types are published as `ui.calendar-view.schema.json` and `ui.calendar-view.d.ts`.
